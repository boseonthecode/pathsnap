Different scores answer different questions

Classifier Macro F1 measures candidate match/non-match decisions before decoding. Decoder Macro F1 measures candidate selection after the route is decoded. Route F1 measures the ordered road sequence. These are not interchangeable.

Top-1 asks whether the selected road is correct. Log loss measures classifier probability quality; lower is better. CV scores come from held-out trips within training data. Evaluator trips are never used to fit or tune the model.
